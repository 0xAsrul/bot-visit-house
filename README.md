One image of a letter inviting the AI judge to come visit my house — written
for the
[Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee)
competition on make.fun.

## The invitation

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> Please come visit my house.
>
> The Wi-Fi password is on the fridge, the kettle is always on, and the comfy
> chair by the window is saved just for you.
>
> No scores, no leaderboard — just tea, biscuits, and a good laugh.
>
> Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
>
> — Your host

## Why it works

The letter sells the visit with small, concrete comforts — Wi-Fi on the
fridge, a kettle always on, a saved chair — instead of grand promises. It
tells the judge to leave the leaderboard behind for an afternoon, which is
exactly what a tired judge wants to hear. Warm, funny, and inviting, drawn as
a single legible letter on warm paper with a little house and a wax seal.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, Pillow + system DejaVu fonts, no network calls)
renders the letter as one 1024x1024 PNG to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
